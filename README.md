# SUNBREAK — Relay 9

An original, offline three.js tactical FPS. Amber **Breakers** and teal **Wardens** contest a coastal energy station: three approaches, a central sun cut, and the Solar and Tidal relay sites. Lead a three-person squad against three bots. Arm a charge, protect it for 36 seconds, or hold E for a five-second defuse. Elimination also wins rounds. First to seven wins; attack and defense switch every three rounds.

Stop before firing: moving widens spread, sustained fire adds recoil, and headshots do 3.5× damage. Bots navigate around cover, hold sites, investigate footsteps and shots, and rotate to armed relays. Round rewards and kill bounties buy three primary weapons, armor and frag grenades. All geometry and WebAudio sounds are generated by the game; three.js is vendored locally.

Run `node serve.ts` (Node 22.18+) and open **http://localhost:8080** in desktop Chrome. No install or build step. Click Deploy for mouse capture; Escape pauses. WASD move, mouse aim, click fire, right click focus, R reload, Ctrl crouch, Shift walk, 1 pistol, 2 primary, G frag, E plant/defuse, B supply, Tab roster. Buy during the 15-second opening phase. The supply screen keeps the round clock running. On death, watch surviving squadmates; click cycles cameras.

Package with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`. This copies `game/`, `screenshots/`, and writes the deterministic dossier using the unmodified kit library. `shots.json` lists repeatable browser screenshot scenes; `?shot=start|play|end&seed=1` freezes the matching stage.

Verification commands and the outstanding browser screenshot capture are documented in [TESTING.md](TESTING.md). `screenshots/` currently has no images; capture the three staged views before packaging for submission.

MIT licensed. No network access is needed to play. The three.js license is included under `game/vendor/three/`.
